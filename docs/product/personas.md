# Personas

> Source: [discovery-log.md](discovery-log.md) Sessions 4–6. Roles below are confirmed unless marked
> `@assumption`. A role that is not listed here must not appear in a story or a Policy.

---

### Student — Học sinh THCS

**Who:** lower-secondary students, grades 6–9, roughly CEFR A1–A2 in English. Minors.
**Technical comfort:** medium · **Devices:** computer, at school or at home
**Uses the system:** several times a week during the trial

**Needs:** learn common communicative sentences for a topic; practise a conversation and get
feedback; review what they forget.
**Must NOT be able to:** see other students' conversations or results; edit topics.
**Scope of data:** their own progress only.
**Pain today:** almost no speaking practice in class, and no partner or feedback at home.

### Teacher — Giáo viên tiếng Anh

**Who:** the English teacher who requested the product; runs the trial with 1–2 classes.
**Technical comfort:** medium · **Devices:** computer
**Uses the system:** weekly

**Needs:** create classes and student accounts; prepare and approve topic content (AI-drafted);
see which sentences students struggle with.
**Must NOT be able to:** see classes that belong to another teacher. `@assumption`
**Scope of data:** their own classes.
**Pain today:** cannot give forty students individual speaking practice and correction.

### Administrator — Quản trị `@assumption`

**Who:** the customer (developer/owner of the entry). Creates teacher accounts.
**Scope of data:** everything.
